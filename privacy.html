Plainly Studio – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Summary. Plainly Studio is a private tool used by one person, the owner of the “Plainly” channels, to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own accounts. It does not collect, sell or share personal information about anyone else.

What is stored. Only the owner's own access tokens for TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, kept on the owner's own computer so that the tool can upload the owner's videos. Nothing is stored about viewers, followers or any other person.

What is shared. Nothing is shared with third parties. The tool only sends the owner's finished videos and captions to the owner's own accounts on those platforms.

Other users. The tool has no other users and no sign-in for the public, so it does not collect data from visitors.

Deleting data. The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in the settings of TikTok, Instagram or Google, and can delete the stored tokens from the computer.
