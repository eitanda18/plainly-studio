Last updated: 27 October 2026

What this is. Plainly Studio is a private tool that the owner of the “Plainly” short-video channels uses to publish the owner's own original videos to the owner's own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts.

Who can use it. Only the owner. The tool is not offered to the public, there are no other users, and it does not ask anyone else to sign in or connect an account.

What it does. It prepares a short educational video and, after the owner approves it, uploads it to the owner's own account. The owner stays in control: uploads to TikTok are sent as drafts that the owner reviews and posts personally.

Content. All videos are produced by the owner. The owner is responsible for the content, and for following the terms and community guidelines of TikTok and the other platforms.

No warranty. The tool is provided as is, for the owner's personal use.
